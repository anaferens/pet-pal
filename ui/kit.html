Navigation

Amber appears here and nowhere else: the band, and the 24×3px marker over the active tab.

Profiles

Seven components need a photograph. Where one hasn't loaded, the slot shows its photo ground — never a grey stand-in the reader is left looking at. People have no portraits yet, so a person's avatar carries initials.

Forms

One button API replaces six drifting names. Every control carries a programmatic label and clears the 44px tap floor.

Reminders

Status colour speaks only about status. Due-soon bronze is deliberately not the signal amber, so a warning is never mistaken for an action.

Cards

The listing row is the most repeated component in the product — a 36px icon tile, a title, and a meta line that must restate its own font or it inherits the heading's 700.

States empty · error · loading

A missing image is the amber-tint illustration tile, never a grey fill. Error copy names what failed and what to do — no apology, and never “Something went wrong”.

Utilities the four that aren't components

Everything else in the kit is a component. These four carry no look of their own — they hand a plain element the canvas inset, the screen-reader-only treatment, the icon-only box, or the name face.
