The shared shell

Two-layer header plus tab bar, as markup. A grey wireframe adopts this structure first and inherits the visual language for free — a CSS-only kit cannot style a skeleton that isn't there. Everything below is kit.css; nothing here is page-specific.

Two layers, two jobs. Layer 1 (.pp-appbar) is the amber band carrying back / title / action — it says where you are. Layer 2 (.pp-hero + .pp-arch) carries the subject: the photograph straddling the arch, then the name. A screen with no subject — a list, a form — uses layer 1 alone and drops layer 2.
