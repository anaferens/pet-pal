🐾 PetPal · Concept · Step 03

Visual direction — Refero synthesis

Credible enough for a vet. Warm enough to hand to a sitter.

August Health sets the foundation — calm, credible healthcare-warmth. We borrow three concrete techniques: Kinhive's serif ↔ italic serif, Headspace's colour discipline, and Airbnb's photography-led warmth on a clean, tidy canvas. Not a copy of any one — one foundation, three techniques.

Sourced via the Refero MCP, grounded in the trust benchmarks and persona anxieties already in research.md and personas.md. No new competitor search. Named benchmarks in play: Headspace, Airbnb, Lassie, MyTherapy, Rover.

Anxieties we design against

Every technique below is chosen to reduce a specific, sourced persona anxiety — the “why,” not decoration.

A1 · Proof

Verbal “he’s up-to-date” isn’t accepted — Eva needs a record a vet, kennel or groomer will believe.personas.md · research.md §F9

A2 · Handoff

56% feel “overwhelming guilt and anxiety” leaving their pet; the handoff is emotionally loaded.research.md §F11

Emergency moment: a busy or alarming screen adds stress at the worst time.personas.md · R5

Styles

One foundation, taken as the mood and density; two borrowed techniques, each a concrete move — not a general impression.

Foundation

August Health EHR

augusthealth.com ↗

The only reference that is literally “healthcare seriousness with cheerful warmth” — the exact balance PetPal’s voice committed to: own trusted, not complete. A senior-care records job close to ours: sensitive data that families and professionals must both trust.

  • Two-surface system, no dividers — white ↔ Warm Sand; sections break by background change, never a rule line.
  • Serif headline + clean sans body — the serif is the credibility cue; the sans keeps records legible. (The roman/italic treatment is Kinhive's borrow.)
  • 16px floating cards radius 16 · no border — soft layered shadow only; fully-pill badges.
  • Real photo in a soft circular mask — already matches PetPal’s dossier hero.
  • One primary accent only — a restrained palette, single action colour.
#f8f3eb sand #4865ff action #328a3b green #ff6d39 accent #080331 ink

A1  the serif + calm clinical-but-warm polish makes the shared card read as a real health record a vet trusts  ·  A2 warmth keeps it from feeling bureaucratic at the handoff.

Borrow #1 · serif + italic serif

Kinhive

kinhive.com ↗

August Health's close sibling — same clean-white + serif family. We take only its type method, not its brand.

  • The type carries the message: an upright roman serif paired with an italic serif in one headline — roman for the credible/clear words, italic for the warm ones. Warmth in the letterforms, before any colour or photo.
  • Live in the master line above — upright "Credible enough for a vet." + italic "Warm enough to hand to a sitter."

A1  roman reads credible / legit to a vet or kennel  ·  A2 italic reads warm at the handoff — one typeface, both jobs.

Borrow #2 · the amount of colour

Headspace

headspace.com ↗

We take the discipline, not the whimsy — Headspace’s playful blobs would undercut credibility.

  • Colour budget: a warm off-white base, one action colour for every interactive element, and saturated hues quarantined to illustrations & icons only — never text blocks or large surfaces.
  • Flat 2px lift 0 2px 0 rgba(65,61,69,.2) instead of heavy shadow.
#f9f4f2 base #0061ef action #ffce00 accent #3b197f plum

A4  a screen that’s 90% calm neutral, colour only where you act, stays quiet under stress and makes the one right tap obvious  ·  A2

Borrow #3 · photography + emotion, clean

Airbnb

airbnb.com ↗

The designer’s warmth pick over Finn — Airbnb communicates emotion better while staying clean and tidy: illustration, warmth, but nothing loud.

  • Let the image do the feeling — an airy, tidy near-white canvas that lets emotional full-bleed imagery (real photography + friendly illustration, never stock) carry the warmth, in rounded ~20px “photo-print” cards with small trust badges.
  • Colour scarcity — a single accent used almost ceremonially, so warmth reads as emotion, not decoration.
#ffffff canvas #ff385c coral #222222 ink #717171 meta

A2  a warm real photo of this pet on a clean card carries the emotion at the handoff  ·  A3 a sitter who never met the animal connects instantly
