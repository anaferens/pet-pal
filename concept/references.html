Visual direction — Refero synthesis

Credible enough for a vet. Warm enough to hand to a sitter.

August Health sets the foundation — calm, credible healthcare-warmth. We borrow exactly two things: Headspace's colour discipline and Finn's warm brown text with real-pet photography. Not a copy of any one — one foundation, two concrete techniques.

Sourced via the Refero MCP, grounded in the trust benchmarks and persona anxieties already in research.md and personas.md. No new competitor search. Named benchmarks in play: Headspace, Airbnb, Lassie, MyTherapy, Rover.

Anxieties we design against

Every technique below is chosen to reduce a specific, sourced persona anxiety — the “why,” not decoration.

A1 · Proof

Verbal “he’s up-to-date” isn’t accepted — Eva needs a record a vet, kennel or groomer will believe.personas.md · research.md §F9

A2 · Handoff

Styles

One foundation, taken as the mood and density; two borrowed techniques, each a concrete move — not a general impression.

Foundation

August Health EHR

augusthealth.com ↗

The only reference that is literally “healthcare seriousness with cheerful warmth” — the exact balance PetPal’s voice committed to: own trusted, not complete. A senior-care records job close to ours: sensitive data that families and professionals must both trust.

  • Two-surface system, no dividers — white ↔ Warm Sand; sections break by background change, never a rule line.
  • Serif headline + clean sans body — the serif is the credibility cue; the sans keeps records legible.
  • 16px floating cards radius 16 · no border — soft layered shadow only; fully-pill badges.
  • Real photo in a soft circular mask — already matches PetPal’s dossier hero.
  • One primary accent only — a restrained palette, single action colour.
#f8f3eb sand #4865ff action #328a3b green #ff6d39 accent #080331 ink

A1  the serif + calm clinical-but-warm polish makes the shared card read as a real health record a vet trusts  ·  A2 warmth keeps it from feeling bureaucratic at the handoff.

Borrow #1 · the amount of colour

Headspace

headspace.com ↗

We take the discipline, not the whimsy — Headspace’s playful blobs would undercut credibility.

  • Colour budget: a warm off-white base, one action colour for every interactive element, and saturated hues quarantined to illustrations & icons only — never text blocks or large surfaces.
  • Flat 2px lift 0 2px 0 rgba(65,61,69,.2) instead of heavy shadow.
#f9f4f2 base #0061ef action #ffce00 accent #3b197f plum

A4  a screen that’s 90% calm neutral, colour only where you act, stays quiet under stress and makes the one right tap obvious  ·  A2

Borrow #2 · warm text + real photography

Finn

petfinn.com ↗

An actual warm-yet-trustworthy pet brand. We keep only the text warmth and photo-as-anchor, not the e-commerce loudness.

  • Warm brown body text #321004 instead of pure black or cool grey — reads human at body size, full contrast kept.
  • The pet’s own photo as the emotional / social-proof anchor — expressive real animals, not stock icons.
#321004 text #161345 primary #feafbb blush #ff7f00 rating

A3  warm copy beside a real photo of this pet feels like a friend’s note, not a cold database — a nervous sitter reads it fast  ·  A2
