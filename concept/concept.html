Palette

primary · accent · neutral · semantic

One signal colour does the emphasis; everything else is ink on white. Each colour is labelled by the attribute — the job it is allowed to do. All pairings meet WCAG AA (text ≥ 4.5:1): near-black on the amber signal reads 11:1, and amber is a fill — never text on white (amber text uses a deep amber-ink #8A6B00).
